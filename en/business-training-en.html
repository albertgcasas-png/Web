FAQ

What everyone asks

Are sessions in person or online?

We offer both. In-person sessions usually work better for groups and initial training; online sessions for follow-ups and individual training.

How many people can be in the group?

We work with groups of between 2 and 15 people. For larger teams, we design a specific format or run sessions in smaller groups.

Do we get materials after the training?

Yes. Every programme includes reference documentation, best practice guides and resources specific to your context.

How is the training result measured?

We define concrete indicators before the training: time spent on certain tasks, errors, adoption speed. We measure them in the weeks that follow.
