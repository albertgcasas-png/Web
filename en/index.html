FAQ

What everyone asks before calling

Straight answers, no marketing.

How long before we see a real result?

In most cases, between 2 and 6 weeks with a well-focused pilot. We start with the improvements that deliver the most immediate impact and scale from there.

Do we need to buy new tools or change what we have?

The first step is always to get the most out of what you already have. When we recommend a new tool, it's because the benefit is clear, concrete and affordable for your business.

Our team isn't very tech-savvy. Will it work?

Yes, that's the most common situation. Training and support are part of every project. Everything we put in place, the team can use and maintain independently.

What if the project delivers less than expected?

We define the success criteria with concrete figures before we start. If results fall short, the project stops at no extra cost and you keep the analysis and recommendations.

What budget should we plan for?

The digital diagnosis is fixed price: €450, deductible if we continue working together. Automation and AI projects range from €800 to €3,500 depending on scope. Always a fixed budget in writing.

Do you work across Spain or only in Girona?

We work across Catalonia and remotely throughout Spain. Most sessions are done by video call; we visit in person when it makes sense for the project.

Is there any commitment to asking for information?

None. The first conversation is free and without any commitment. If we see we can bring you real value, we'll explain how. If your case needs a different profile, we'll tell you clearly and point you in the right direction.
